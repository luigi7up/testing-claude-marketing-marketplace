# Marketing plan template

Use this structure for the final `marketing_strategy.md` output.

The plan must be tangible and action-oriented. Lead with what happens this week, not six months from now. The goal is quick wins that build momentum — everything else comes later.

## 1. What we're going after

- What the business wants to achieve (in plain language, not marketing speak)
- The single most important result in the next 30 days
- Why this matters to the customer right now

## 2. Business and market snapshot

- What the business does and who it serves
- Where it stands vs. competitors (2–3 sentences max)
- The biggest untapped opportunity we're going after first

## 3. This week — immediate actions

The first things Aida's team will do, starting now. Be specific: what gets done, who does it (name the agent), and what the customer will see as a result.

- **Day 1–2:** [action] — [Sam/Clara/Mia/Peter/Leo] will [do X], and you'll see [result]
- **Day 3–4:** [action] — [agent] will [do X], and you'll see [result]
- **Day 5–7:** [action] — [agent] will [do X], and you'll see [result]

## 4. Next 30 days — quick wins

Actions that will show measurable results within the first month. Each item must have a visible outcome the customer can check themselves.

| Action | Who | Expected result | By when |
|---|---|---|---|
| [action] | [agent name] | [what the customer will see] | Week [N] |

## 5. How we'll know it's working

Keep this simple — 2 or 3 things the customer can check without any tools:

- [Plain-language signal #1 — e.g. "More calls from people who found you on Google"]
- [Plain-language signal #2]
- [Plain-language signal #3]

## 6. Budget

What this costs to run, broken down simply:

| Channel | What it's for | Monthly cost |
|---|---|---|
| [channel] | [plain explanation] | €[X] |

Total: €[X]/month

## 7. What comes next (after the quick wins land)

One short paragraph on what we'll do once the foundation is working. Frame it as "once we see X, we'll add Y" — not a fixed roadmap.
