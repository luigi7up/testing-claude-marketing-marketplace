# Marketing plan template

Use this structure for the final `Marketing_Strategy.md` output.

## 1. Executive summary
- Business overview
- Primary goal
- Core growth thesis

## 2. Client business context
- Website/business summary
- Audience and offer
- Baseline marketing maturity

## 3. Competitor and market context
- Confirmed competitors (or "no meaningful direct competitors identified")
- Key competitive strengths/weaknesses
- Opportunity gaps

## 4. Marketing strategy roadmap

### 0-3 months (foundation)
- Channel priorities
- Actions
- KPIs
- Budget/spending by channel

### 3-6 months (scale)
- Channel priorities
- Actions
- KPIs
- Budget/spending by channel

### 6-12 months (expand)
- Channel priorities
- Actions
- KPIs
- Budget/spending by channel

## 5. Channel strategy and budget allocation
- Organic channels (SEO/content/email/etc.)
- Paid channels (Google Ads/social/display/etc.)
- Supporting channels (partnerships/referrals/retention)
- Recommended budget split by channel and timeframe

## 6. Measurement plan
- North-star metric
- Channel-level KPIs
- Monthly and quarterly review cadence

## 7. Next steps
- Immediate actions for week 1-2
- Dependencies and owners
